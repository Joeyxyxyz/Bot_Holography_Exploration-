    Safety

    • STOP, the Esc key, or Ctrl+C in the studio's console stops at once: the projector goes dark and the rig is released.
    • The projector is driven over HDMI only. The studio never changes LEDs, modes or firmware.
    • Only one program uses the projector and camera at a time (shared with the scanner).
    • Don't look into the projector lens. Moving the case, focus and cables are Joey's.
